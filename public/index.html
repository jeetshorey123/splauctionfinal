Match Rules

Match Rules (Applicable to ALL Matches)

  1. No mini overs are allowed under any circumstances.
  2. No over can be declared incomplete once one or more legal deliveries have been bowled.
  3. The umpire's decision is final in all match situations.
  4. Only the team captain is allowed to speak with the umpire or committee.
  5. Only the captain may appeal; no other player is permitted to do so.

2. Match Format

2.1 League Matches

  • Each match will be 5 overs per side.
  • Each bowler is allowed to bowl only 1 over.

2.2 Playoff Matches

  • Each match will be 6 overs per side.
  • Only one bowler per team is allowed to bowl a maximum of 2 overs.

3. Bowling Rules

3.1 Bowling Action & Position

  1. The bowler must deliver the ball from within the bowling crease.
  2. The bowler's foot must not touch or cross any crease line, no ball if it does.
  3. Even if the shoe is not touching the line but is ahead of it, it will be considered a No Ball.
  4. Bowler hits the stump while bowling is a no ball.
  5. 1 check allowed per over, 2nd check is a no ball.
  6. The bowler must inform the umpire of the side from which they are bowling.
  7. Side-arm bowling above waist height is not allowed – direct No Ball (no warning).
  8. Bowling above the prescribed speed limit is a direct No Ball, same limit for all batsmen.

3.2 No-Ball Rules

A delivery will be called a No Ball if:

  1. The ball pitches in the first half of the pitch, i.e., behind the white mid-line (Line touch is also a No Ball).
  2. A full toss above knee height (inside the crease).
  3. Bowler hits the stump while bowling is a no ball.
  4. 1 check allowed per over, 2nd check is a no ball.
  5. Side-arm bowling above waist height is not allowed – direct No Ball (no warning).
  6. Bowling above the prescribed speed limit is a direct No Ball, same limit for all batsmen.
  7. The ball pitches twice outside the batting crease.
  8. The keeper collects the ball ahead of the stumps.
  9. More than the permitted number of fielders are positioned behind the stumps.
  10. The ball passes above stump height and the batsman misses the ball, OR the ball passes outside the pitch area.
  11. If the ball passes at stump height from the side, it is NOT a No Ball unless the ball goes outside the pitch without touching the keeper.

3.3 Batsman Advancing / Leaving the Crease

  • If the batsman leaves the crease or advances forward:
    • If the ball hits the bat or the body, it is a FAIR DELIVERY, regardless of height.
    • If the batsman misses the ball and the ball passes above stump height, it is a No Ball.

3.4 Dead Ball & High Ball Rule

  1. A ball bowled above head height is a Dead Ball.
  2. If the same bowler bowls above head height again, the second instance is a No Ball.

4. Batting Rules

  1. Tap and run is allowed anywhere in front of the stumps.
  2. Intentional double-tap by the batsman is OUT (Double Hit).
  3. On a No Ball: Run out is valid, Stumping is NOT OUT.
  4. A batsman will be declared OUT on a No Ball if the ball is hit above the box restriction (The ball does not count, 1 run is awarded to the batting team).
  5. Shots played behind the stumps are treated as dot Ball, unless the ball spins back from in front of the stumps.

5. Fielding Rules

5.1 Fielding Restrictions

  1. Only 4 fielders are allowed behind the stumps on the boundary.
  2. If extra fielders are present behind the stumps → No Ball.
  3. 90-degree fielding is allowed.
  4. Fake fielding is OUT (umpire's decision).
  5. Obstructing the field is OUT (umpire's decision).

5.2 Substitute & Injury Rules

  1. An extra fielder is allowed only if a player is injured.
  2. Rotation point will be granted only if injury occurs during the match.
  3. Protection guards are allowed but must not stop the ball or give advantage.

6. Boundary & Playing Area Rules

6.1 Boundary Definition

  1. The white line is the official boundary.
  2. Curtain on square boundary and nets act as boundary indicators.

6.2 Boundary Outcomes

  • Straight Boundary:
    • Ball touching the ribbon → 6 runs
    • Ball above the ribbon → OUT
  • Square Boundary:
    • Ball touching the curtain directly → OUT

Final contact decides the result, regardless of initial height. (boundary umpire decision is final)

6.3 Box Restriction

  1. The second white strip on square boundary is the box restriction line.
  2. Any ball hit on or above this line is OUT.

6.4 Special Ball Situations

  1. Ball hits net behind keeper → Play continues.
  2. Ball tears net and passes through → 1D+
  3. Ball stuck inside grill → 1D
  4. Ball hits grill and comes back → Play continues.
  5. Ball going outside playing area:
    • Directly above chairs/tables → Dot Ball
    • Hits a tap → 1D
    • Touches tables → 1D

7. Runs, Overthrows & Dismissals

  1. Overthrow runs are valid.
  2. Bails must fall at striker's end.
  3. If stump is moved or touched, it must be removed before judging run-out.
  4. Shuffle wide: Wide is given if the ball does not hit bat or stumps, irrespective of batsman stance.

8. Challenge Over Rule

  1. The 2nd over of every match is a Challenge Over.
  2. 5 fielders are allowed on the boundary.
  3. Batting team target: 16 runs.
    • If achieved → Runs × 2
    • If not → Runs ÷ 2 (rounded off)

9. Youngster / Impact Player Rule

  1. Each team will have one Impact Player (youngest player).
  2. Runs scored by the Impact Player are multiplied by 2.

10. Team Composition & Attendance

  1. Maximum 10 players per team.
  2. 10 players must be present on the field.
  3. If any player does not show up: 1-point swap bonus will not be awarded.
  4. If informed one day prior, captain may choose an extra player.
  5. Deadline: 12:00 PM, 24th January.
  6. All players must report by 10:00 AM, 25th January.

11. Points System

11.1 Match Points

  • Win → 2 points
  • Loss → 0 points

11.2 Bonus Points

  • Win with changed playing 10 → 3 points
  • Loss with changed playing 10 → 1 point
  • Captain must inform committee & scorer at toss.

11.3 Penalty

  • 5-run penalty per league match for: Valiant, Strikers, Legends

12. Tournament Format

12.1 League Stage

  • Each team plays at least once against every other team.
  • Top 5 teams qualify based on Points & NRR.

12.2 Playoffs

  • 1st ranked team qualifies directly to Final.
  • Next 4 teams play round-robin.
  • Last-ranked playoff team is eliminated.

13. Super Over Rules

  1. No Super Over in league matches → 1 point each.
  2. In playoffs:
    • Super Over played
    • If tied → Team with most boundaries wins
    • If tied again → Another Super Over (No same batter or bowler allowed)
    • If still tied → Toss decides winner

14. Discipline & Ground Conduct

  1. No player allowed to:
    • Sit in front of nets
    • Sit on boundary sitting stands
    • Cross the rope as a viewer
  2. Players must sit only in allocated team seats.
  3. Only captains & committee members allowed on field for discussions.
  4. One player must be assigned next to the scorer.
  5. Keeper change to leg umpire must be informed or it is a No Ball.
  6. Tracks and team jerseys are compulsory (Exception allowed for extra player).
